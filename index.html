Clinical pocket guide

Orthopaedic Oncology

For clinician education only. Verify management decisions with current institutional protocols, specialty guidance, and attending judgment. Do not enter PHI.

Mirels score

A commonly used framework for estimating pathologic fracture risk in long-bone metastatic disease. Clinical context still matters.

Imaging workflow

Start with orthogonal radiographs for osseous lesions. MRI characterizes marrow/soft-tissue extent; CT better defines cortical detail and mineralization. Stage suspected malignancy before biopsy when feasible.

Biopsy principle

Biopsy trajectory should be planned with the definitive surgical team so the tract can be excised with the specimen and critical compartments are not contaminated.

Red flags

Escalate new neurovascular deficit, rapidly progressive pain/swelling, threatened skin, fever with wound concerns, or suspected pathologic fracture.

Documentation

For limb exams, document skin/incision, tenderness/mass, ROM, motor groups, sensation by named distributions when possible, distal perfusion, and relevant gait or assistive device.
